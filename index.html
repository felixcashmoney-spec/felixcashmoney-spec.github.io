Strange Knowns

Things we know that still sound strange.

Strange Knowns is an English-language knowledge channel on YouTube and TikTok about space, history and psychology.

YouTube Privacy Policy

How the videos are made

Scripts, narration, images and clips are produced with the help of AI tools and published automatically. Every video is checked by an automated review before it goes online. Each description says that script and voice were made with AI.

Strange Knowns Pipeline

The Strange Knowns Pipeline is a private tool used only by the operator of the channel. It signs in with Google to:

  • upload videos to the Strange Knowns YouTube channel and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing times,
  • read the statistics of the channel's own videos through the YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs,
  • read public information about other YouTube channels for topic research.

It is not offered to anyone else and does not access any other user's account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
